Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

"my-video-uploader" connects only to the TikTok account of its owner, using TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. It stores the owner's access token on the owner's own computer to upload the owner's videos.

It does not collect, store, or share any personal data from other TikTok users or from viewers. No analytics, cookies, or third-party services are used on this site.

To revoke access, remove the app from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings.
